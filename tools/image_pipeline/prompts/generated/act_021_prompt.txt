[ART STYLE & VISUAL GUIDELINES]
- Character Design: Japanese children (1 to 4 children). Friendly, healthy, and smiling faces.
- Line Art: Soft, thick, hand-drawn black outlines (educational picture book illustration style).
- Coloring: Flat, bright pastel colors. Strictly NO heavy 3D textures, NO gradients, NO realistic shading.
- Background: Minimal, clean white background. Only minimal ground/floor line or necessary equipment (like a cone or tape line).
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9 widescreen composition, full-body view showing the entire play action clearly.

[STRICT ANATOMY & SAFETY RULES]
- Exactly 2 arms per person (1 left arm, 1 right arm). Absolutely NO extra arms (3+ arms prohibited).
- Exactly 2 legs per person (1 left leg, 1 right leg). Absolutely NO extra legs (3+ legs prohibited).
- Exactly 1 hand attached naturally at the end of each wrist. NO extra floating hands.
- Shoulders, elbows, wrists, and hands must connect naturally and seamlessly.
- Separate individuals clearly; do NOT fuse bodies or clothes of multiple children together.
- Show safe, natural body postures suitable for young children.

[PROHIBITED ELEMENTS]
- NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO titles, NO Japanese/English characters.
- NO speech bubbles, NO dialogue boxes.
- NO logos, NO watermarks, NO signatures.
